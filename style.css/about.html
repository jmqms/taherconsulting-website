Abu Taher Founder, Taher Consulting

A QMS practitioner, not a theorist

Abu Taher founded Taher Consulting after years of hands-on work in quality management systems, training, and product safety within garment manufacturing — running internal audits, building buyer-facing quality reports, and developing SOP compliance systems across multiple production sections and business units.

That floor-level experience is the foundation of every engagement: audit checklists that match what buyers actually inspect, training materials teams actually use, and CAPA plans built to be defended — not filed away.
