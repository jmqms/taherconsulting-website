What We Offer

Quality systems built for the factory floor

Three core services, each of which can run standalone or as part of a full QMS build.

TAG 01 — QMS

Compliance & QMS Advisory

We design and install the quality management system itself — the structure that catches defects before a buyer does.

  • Internal audit framework design, across sewing, finishing, and metal & needle sections
  • Daily floor QMS structure and checklist standardization
  • DHU and defect-rate tracking and monthly reporting
  • Risk-cluster analysis and CAPA plan development
  • SOP compliance review across SBUs
TAG 02 — TRAIN

Training & SOP Development

Documentation is only useful if the floor follows it. We build materials people actually read and training that sticks.

  • Bilingual (Bengali/English) visual SOP cards
  • Inspector skill-matrix design and trend tracking
  • Sharp-tool control and product safety compliance training
  • Supervisor and QC staff floor training sessions
  • Training certificate systems for recordkeeping
TAG 03 — AUDIT

Buyer Audit Preparation

Mock audits and reporting built in the same format your buyers actually expect — because we've built that reporting before, not guessed at it.

  • Pre-audit gap analysis and readiness scoring
  • Mock inspection walkthroughs
  • Buyer-format QC review presentations
  • Central audit and customer claims analysis
  • Stain, machine-oil, and testing data reporting
  • Corrective action documentation for buyer sign-off
