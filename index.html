How An Engagement Runs

Four stages, start to handover

01 / PLAN

Floor Assessment

We walk the lines, review your current SOPs and inspection records, and map where defects and non-conformities are actually coming from.

02 / BUILD

System Design

QMS structure, audit checklists, and training materials are built around your SBUs — not a generic template.

03 / TRAIN

Floor Rollout

Supervisors and QC staff are trained directly on the new system, in Bengali and English, until it runs without us in the room.

04 / VERIFY

Audit Readiness Check

A mock audit confirms the system holds up, with a final report and CAPA plan for anything still open.
